50,000 UGX — one-time on first application. Settled before any other ledger. Exemptions: HTC, Sponsorship, Existing Student.

$200 USD semester entry + $50 registration = $250. Must clear before student is marked Registered. Unlocks LMS & class access.

USD 750/semester. 50% required for Term 1 assessment access. 100% for Term 2. Discount applied to final installment only.
